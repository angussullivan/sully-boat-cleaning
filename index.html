What we do

Your boat’s performance starts underwater.

We assess the hull and underwater gear, then choose the right tools and process for the vessel, coating condition and level of marine growth.

01

Hull cleaning

Controlled removal of marine growth using tools selected to protect the hull surface and antifouling system.

02

Running gear

Careful cleaning around propellers, shafts, rudders, trim tabs, intakes and other underwater fittings.

03

Underwater checks

A methodical visual check of the below-waterline hull and accessible underwater components while we work.

04

Scheduled care

Practical maintenance intervals based on your vessel, location, use and local growth conditions.

Safety, care & compliance

Professional work under the surface.

Underwater hull care demands trained people and a disciplined process. Our two-person team brings commercial diving and safety qualifications to every job, with an approach designed to protect your vessel and the surrounding marine environment.

  • Commercially qualified and safety-trained divers
  • Correct tool selection for the hull, coating and growth
  • Environmental and site requirements built into the job
  • Two-diver procedures and clear pre-work assessment

Straightforward pricing

Complete care, priced by boat length.

Prices are in Australian dollars and cover a standard monohull with safe marina or pontoon access and light-to-moderate fouling. Final pricing is confirmed before work begins.

Every complete clean includes

  • Hull from waterline to keel
  • Keel, rudder and accessible running gear
  • Propeller, shaft, trim tabs and thrusters where fitted
  • Accessible intakes and through-hulls
  • Visual anode assessment
  • Before-and-after photos and condition report
